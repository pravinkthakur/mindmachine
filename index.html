The problem

Most technology estates are a black box.

Decades of in-house development, outsourced builds and changing teams have left most organisations running systems nobody fully understands. Without that understanding it's hard to judge what to keep, what to fix, what to replace — or how to brief the partner who'll build what comes next.

01 / KNOWLEDGE GAP

Decades of change, little memory

The people who built it have moved on. The documentation, if it exists, describes a system that no longer does.

02 / HARD TO ASSESS

Secure, robust, fit for purpose?

How relevant is your code base? How exposed? How connected to everything around it? Most leaders can't say with confidence.

03 / AI AMPLIFIES IT
